01

Level 1 · Starter

The Foundation

Free Start for free

Who it's for

You have talent and clients, but your income goes up and down from one month to the next. You say yes to everything, set your prices by gut feeling and don't really know what's making you money.

What you'll learn

  1. Assess your current offer
    Find where your money leaks every month and what you deliver vs what you actually sell.
  2. Understand your model
    One-off project, package, retainer: choose the model that lets you grow.
  3. Structure your offer
    A clear offer that's easy to understand and easy to buy.
  4. Sell with clarity
    A simple process to go from inquiry to signed contract.

By the end of this level

  • You know exactly what you sell and to whom
  • You can spot the 3 signs your price is too low
  • You have an offer ready to pitch
02

Level 2 · Intermediate

The Value

In progress Get notified when it opens

Who it's for

Your offer is clear, but you're still charging too little. You work a lot for what you earn and you want clients who pay for your expertise, not your hours.

What you'll learn

  1. Raise your perceived value
    Positioning, presentation and proof: what justifies a higher price.
  2. Set your prices
    Move from an hourly rate to value-based pricing.
  3. Land retainers
    Turn one-off projects into recurring revenue.
  4. Negotiate without discounting
    Handle price objections without sacrificing your margin.

By the end of this level

  • You charge what you're really worth
  • You have at least one recurring offer
  • Your income becomes predictable
03

Level 3 · Advanced

The Scale

Coming soon Notify me at launch

Who it's for

Your business is running, but everything rests on you. You want to grow without working more, and stop being the bottleneck of your own company.

What you'll learn

  1. Build your systems
    Document your processes so they run without you.
  2. Delegate
    What to hand off, to whom, and how to keep the quality.
  3. Steer with numbers
    The metrics to track to make better decisions.

By the end of this level

  • Your business runs without your constant presence
  • You spend your time on what really matters
